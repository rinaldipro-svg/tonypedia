The 2026 Robotics Mandate

The robotics economy is no longer a single sector — it is the backbone of five industries simultaneously. From surgical systems replacing human hands in operating rooms to autonomous mining trucks running 24/7 without drivers, the deployment curve has inflected. Industry 5.0 is defined by human-robot collaboration at scale.

The clearest signals: Intuitive Surgical at all-time highs with a fortress balance sheet. Caterpillar and Epiroc outperforming on infrastructure spending. Schneider Electric and GXO riding the data center and outsourced automation wave. The risk capital belongs in high-growth names like Symbotic, PROCEPT, and AutoStore — sized appropriately.
